# Fuel Budget Planner v1.0.0
## Track your monthly fuel spending in Sri Lanka

A mobile-first web app that helps you stay on top of your monthly fuel budget using current Sri Lankan fuel prices.

## Highlights
- Set a monthly fuel budget in LKR and log liters used
- See total cost, remaining budget, and affordable liters left in real time
- Budget usage progress bar with clear status: On Track · Approaching Limit · Budget Critical
- Choose from Petrol 92, Petrol 95, Auto Diesel, and Super Diesel
- Your inputs are saved locally (no account required)
- Dark mode friendly, finance-style dashboard UI
